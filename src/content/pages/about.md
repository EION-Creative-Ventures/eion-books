EION Books started as a home for children's stories. Publishing our own titles taught us every step: planning, illustration, layout, covers, ISBNs, and the platforms where books are sold.

Along the way, other authors started asking for help. Today, EION Books offers that experience as a service, so independent authors can publish professionally while keeping full ownership of their work.

We guide and support. You publish through your own accounts, you keep your rights, and your royalties go straight to you.

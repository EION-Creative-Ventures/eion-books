EION Books is a brand of EION Creative Ventures, LLC, based in Norfolk, Virginia. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, who can access it, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

We collect the information you choose to send us through our Start Your Project form. The form is used for publishing services inquiries. It asks for:

- Your name and email address (required)
- Your book's working title, if you share it
- The type of book, where you are in the process, the services you're interested in, the option you're considering, and your ideal timeline
- Anything you write in the message box

If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Please don't include passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information in the form.

## How we use it

We use your information to review your project, reply to you, and, if it's helpful, schedule a consultation and prepare a recommendation or quote. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

## How submissions are stored and delivered

Our website is hosted by Netlify. When you submit the form, Netlify processes and stores your submission and sends a copy by email to our business inbox. Our business email is provided by Google Workspace, so a copy of your submission is also stored in that inbox.

Access to submissions is limited to authorized EION personnel and to the service providers we need for website hosting and business communications, currently Netlify and Google. These providers handle information under their own privacy policies: [Netlify Privacy Policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). They may process information in the United States and other countries.

## How long we keep it

We keep project inquiries for up to two years. This applies both to submissions stored in Netlify and to the related notification emails in our business inbox. At least twice a year, we review stored inquiries and delete those older than two years from both places. If you become a client, we keep the information needed to deliver and support your project for as long as we're working together and as required for our business records.

You can ask us to delete your inquiry sooner at any time.

## Spam protection

To reduce spam, the form includes a hidden field that automated programs tend to fill in, and Netlify automatically screens submissions for signs of spam. This screening may use technical information sent with your submission, such as your IP address, browser details and the content of the form.

## Cookies, analytics and tracking

Our public pages do not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and they do not include embedded content from other services, such as videos or social media feeds. Our fonts are hosted on our own website.

Like nearly every website host, Netlify automatically records basic technical information when pages are requested, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. Netlify uses this information to deliver the website and keep it secure.

Our private editing dashboard is used only by invited EION editors and uses its own sign-in tools. It is not part of the public website.

## Links to other websites

Our pages link to other websites, including bookstores such as Payhip, Amazon and Barnes & Noble, author websites, and other EION Creative Ventures websites. When you visit those sites, their own privacy policies apply, not this one.

## Children and young authors

This website is intended for adults. Our form is meant for authors, and for parents or guardians on behalf of young authors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Our website may recognize young authors whose work we've published, for example in an anthology. When we do, we limit identifying information about minors: we show their names in connection with their published work and do not publish photos, ages, schools or contact details. A parent or guardian can ask us at any time to correct or remove a young author's name or information, and we'll take care of it promptly.

## Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you've sent us. Email us at [eionbookspublishing@paulaybanks.com](mailto:eionbookspublishing@paulaybanks.com) and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the effective date.
